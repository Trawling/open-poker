Open Poker is a free, open-source No-Limit Texas Hold'em app in the spirit
of Lichess: no ads, no tracking, no real money — and a promise that it will
stay that way forever.

Features:
• Quick play against other players or bots — no account needed
• Cash games at several stakes levels, and no-rebuy Sit & Go tournaments
  with rising blinds
• Provably fair deck shuffling — the algorithm is open and verifiable
• Table chat, hand history, live stats
• Self-hosted: the app isn't tied to any single server — point it at your
  own (see the "⚙️ Server address" setting on the home screen) or deploy
  your own in minutes using the instructions in the repository

This is NOT a gambling app and involves no real-money wagering — chips are
virtual and worthless outside the game. Source code is fully open under the
AGPL-3.0 license: https://github.com/Trawling/open-poker
